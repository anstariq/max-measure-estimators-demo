Every project deserves

Who we are

A dedicated estimating team for builders

Max Measure Estimators is a construction cost estimating firm providing reliable, accurate and affordable estimating services to contractors, builders and construction companies across the United States.

Our estimators come from construction, engineering and project-management backgrounds. We understand the complexities of estimating — residential, commercial, industrial and civil — and we work collaboratively so each estimate benefits from the team’s collective experience.

“We help construction companies reduce overhead, sharpen their bids and win more projects.”

Our mission

To empower construction companies with precise, timely and cost-effective estimating — helping clients reduce overhead, improve their bidding process and win more work.

Our vision

To be a leading provider of construction cost estimating services in the United States, known for precision, reliability and dedication to client success.
