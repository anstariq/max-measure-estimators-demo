FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before you send your first set of plans.

What is a construction takeoff?

A construction takeoff is a measured list of the quantities needed to build a project — counts, lengths, areas and volumes taken from the drawings. Max Measure Estimators prepares accurate quantity takeoffs for every trade, organised by CSI division, with marked-up plan sets so the numbers are ready to price.

What is the difference between a takeoff and a bid estimate?

A takeoff measures the quantities on the plans. A bid estimate goes further: it is a complete, bid-ready estimate with material, labor and pricing, delivered as Excel and PDF files, with bid proposal support. If you are not sure which you need, tell us about the project and the deadline and we will recommend the right scope — a takeoff only, or a full priced estimate.

What services does Max Measure Estimators offer?

Max Measure Estimators provides construction cost estimating and material takeoffs for general contractors and subcontractors. Services include quick takeoffs, preliminary estimates, bid estimates, change order estimates, and material and labor cost analysis. Union and prevailing wage pricing is available on request.

Who do you work with?

We work with general contractors, subcontractors, builders and construction companies of every size across the United States. General contractors receive the full picture; subcontractors receive exactly their scope, including MEP.

Which trades do you estimate?

We cover all CSI divisions — including concrete, masonry, structural steel, lumber, thermal and moisture protection, roofing, doors and windows, drywall, finishes, flooring, insulation, fire suppression, plumbing, mechanical, electrical and sitework.

Which sectors do you cover?

Residential (single-family, additions, remodels and renovations), commercial (hotels, restaurants, education, public facilities and more), industrial and civil/infrastructure projects.

Do you work nationwide?

Yes. We estimate projects across the United States, with pricing tailored to regional labor rates, material costs and building codes. That includes California, Texas, Florida, New York, New Jersey, Georgia, Connecticut, Delaware, Indiana, Kentucky and Maine, and contractors of every size in every state.

Do you offer union or prevailing wage pricing?

Yes. Union and prevailing wage pricing is available on request. Labor pricing can reflect union or prevailing wage requirements for your project’s location — ask when you request a quote.

What software do you use?

Our estimators measure quantities with professional tools such as Bluebeam, PlanSwift and PlanDuct, and price materials and labor using the project’s zip code.

What files can I send?

PDF plan sets are most common, and we can also work from CAD or BIM files. Send what you have along with your scope and bid date.

How does your estimating process work?

There are four steps. You send your plans, scope and bid date. We review the set and confirm price and delivery time before work starts. Our estimators then run a detailed takeoff and cost breakdown for your trades. You receive an Excel and PDF estimate with material, labor and pricing, plus help with revisions.

How fast will I get my estimate?

Most estimates are delivered in 24–48 hours. Large or complex projects may take longer — we’ll confirm the delivery date in your quote before work begins.

What do I receive with my estimate?

You receive an Excel and PDF estimate with material quantities, labor and pricing by line item, organised by CSI division. Materials and labor are priced to the project zip code, and quantities are measured in Bluebeam, PlanSwift and similar tools. Small revisions are included, and we help with bid proposals and budgeting.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on the project size and the trades involved. Send your plans and we’ll reply with a quote that clearly lists the price and delivery time — no hidden charges. Monthly and hourly packages are available for contractors with regular bidding needs.

Do you include revisions?

Yes. Small revisions are included at no charge. Larger scope changes may be quoted separately.

How do I request a quote?

Send your plans through the quote form on our Contact page, email them to info@mmestimators.com, or call (408) 689-8169. Include your drawings — PDF, CAD or BIM — along with your scope and bid date. We review the set and confirm price and delivery time before any work starts. Large plan sets can be sent as a share link.

How do I pay?

Payments are made online by credit card, debit card or PayPal once you’ve approved the quote.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Your plans and personal details are kept confidential and used only to prepare your estimate.
