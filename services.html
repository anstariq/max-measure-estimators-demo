Services

Estimates that win bids
and protect margins

Takeoffs and cost estimates tailored to the way construction companies actually bid — by trade, by phase, and by deadline.

01

Quick Takeoffs

A quick takeoff is a measured list of the quantities needed to build a project, taken from your plans and organised so they are ready to price. Max Measure Estimators prepares accurate quantity takeoffs for every trade, organised by CSI division, with counts, lengths, areas, volumes and marked-up plan sets.

  • All CSI divisions
  • Counts, lengths, areas, volumes
  • Marked-up plan sets
02

Preliminary Estimates

A preliminary estimate is an early-stage budget prepared from schematic or partial drawings, so owners and builders can decide with numbers rather than hunches. Max Measure Estimators provides conceptual and schematic budgets, feasibility and go/no-go figures, and value-engineering options.

  • Conceptual & schematic budgets
  • Feasibility & go/no-go
  • Value-engineering options
03

Bid Estimates

A bid estimate is a complete, bid-ready estimate with material, labor and pricing, built to hit your submission deadline. You receive detailed line-item pricing as Excel and PDF deliverables, along with bid proposal support.

  • Detailed line-item pricing
  • Excel / PDF deliverables
  • Bid proposal support
04

Change Order Estimates

A change order estimate provides fast, defensible pricing for scope changes, so modifications are documented and paid for correctly. Max Measure Estimators prices added or deleted scope, compares revisions, and prepares backup for negotiations.

  • Added / deleted scope
  • Revision comparisons
  • Backup for negotiations
05

Material & Labor Cost Analysis

Material and labor cost analysis is an in-depth breakdown of material and labor costs, adjusted to your project’s location and labor conditions. Pricing is based on the project zip code and includes labor hours and rates, with union or prevailing wage pricing available on request.

  • Zip-code-based pricing
  • Labor hours & rates
  • Union / prevailing wage on request
