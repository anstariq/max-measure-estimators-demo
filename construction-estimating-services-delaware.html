Public work

How do prevailing wages work on Delaware public projects?

On many state-funded public works contracts, Delaware law requires the bid documents to state minimum wages for laborers and mechanics. Under 29 Del. C. § 6960, those specifications apply to a contract or aggregate of contracts for a public works project in excess of $500,000 for new construction (including painting and decorating), or $45,000 for alteration, repair, renovation, rehabilitation, demolition or reconstruction, when the State or a subdivision of the State is a party and the State has appropriated any part of the funds.

Those minimum wages are the rates determined by the Delaware Department of Labor, Division of Industrial Affairs, as prevailing in the county where the work is performed. The Office of Construction Enforcement, part of that Division, administers prevailing-wage compliance on public projects.

Separately, the federal Davis-Bacon and Related Acts apply to federally funded or assisted contracts in excess of $2,000 for the construction, alteration or repair of public buildings or public works. Those U.S. Department of Labor rates are not the same as Delaware’s county determinations. If your bid calls for Delaware prevailing wage, Davis-Bacon, union rates, or more than one of these, tell us when you send the plans. We price labor to the basis you specify; we do not decide whether a project is covered.
